# Stillroom Website

The public website for Stillroom’s studio, work, services, and editorial content.

## Language

**Work Project**:
A portfolio entry that presents Stillroom’s work for a client.
_Avoid_: Case study, work item

**Journal Article**:
An editorial entry published in the Stillroom Journal.
_Avoid_: Blog post, post

**Client Logo**:
The brand identity mark of a client whose work is presented by Stillroom.
_Avoid_: Concept brand, client brand
